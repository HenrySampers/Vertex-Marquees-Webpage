Privacy Notice

Last updated: 24 August 2026

How we use your information

Vertex Marquees collects the name, contact details and event information you submit through the quote form so that we can respond to your enquiry and prepare a quote. We do not use quote enquiries for unrelated marketing.

Who receives it

Quote enquiries are received by Vertex Marquees through Netlify, which hosts this website, and Gmail, which receives the enquiry email.

How long we keep it

We keep unsuccessful quote enquiries for up to 12 months. Booking and payment records are kept only for as long as needed to manage the booking and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Your rights and contact

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or ask questions about how we use it. Contact Vertex Marquees at VertexMarquees@gmail.com or 083 320 5052. You can also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.
